## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for early childhood education, specifically 5-year-old preschoolers embarking on their journey to master Vietnamese letters, diacritics, and numbers. The emotional core centers on joy, curiosity, autonomy, and gentle encouragement. The experience avoids sterile academic seriousness in favor of a tactile, gamified playground where mistakes are soft and wins are delightfully celebrated.

The aesthetic blends **Tactile / Toy-etic Skeuomorphism** with **Playful Flat Cartoon** elements:
- Chunky, pushable interactive components with extruded 3D bevels that invite small fingers to tap.
- High-contrast, warm, and cheerful chromatic accents to stimulate attention while maintaining visual legibility.
- Soft gloss highlights, cloud-like containers, and warm organic boundaries that evoke vinyl toys and illustrated picture books.
- Generous, oversized touch targets designed deliberately for developing motor coordination and tablet usage.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high visual vibrancy, child engagement, and accessibility, using a warm paper-cream background instead of harsh digital whites to prevent visual fatigue during prolonged tablet sessions.

### Core Palette
- **Primary (Sun Yellow - `#FFB800`)**: The anchor of energy and positivity. Used for primary milestone actions, celebratory stars, and active states. Deep extruded base: `#D99B00`.
- **Secondary (Sky Blue - `#38B6FF`)**: Evokes open skies, calm reassurance, and informational containers. Deep extruded base: `#1C8CD9`.
- **Tertiary (Mint Green - `#2BD980`)**: Represents growth, correct answers, success prompts, and audio play triggers. Deep extruded base: `#1A9E58`.
- **Neutral (Warm Cocoa - `#4A3B32`)**: Replaces cold black or charcoal for all high-contrast typography, glyph strokes, and thick borders. Keeps the interface soft, organic, and friendly.

### Expressive & Semantic Accents
- **Candy Pink (`#FF6584`)**: Special achievements, favorite markers, and phonetic tone indicators. Extruded base: `#D94160`.
- **Coral Orange (`#FF7A45`)**: Interactive action cards, drag-and-drop letter tokens, and attention callouts. Extruded base: `#D95320`.
- **Gentle Lavender (`#9D7BFF`)**: Number exploration, magical hints, and secondary category markers. Extruded base: `#7A54E3`.
- **Canvas Cream (`#FFFBF0`)**: Default background surface.
- **Card Cloud Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white elevated panels surrounded by warm borders.

## Typography

**Be Vietnam Pro** is selected for its native typographic engineering for Vietnamese tone marks (dấu hỏi, ngã, sắc, huyền, nặng) and horn/breve letters (ă, â, đ, ê, ô, ơ, ư). Its geometric warmth, large x-height, and open counters ensure young readers instantly recognize complex diacritical configurations without visual crowding.

### Typographic Principles
- **Diacritical Clarity**: All line heights are calibrated 10–15% taller than standard web text to give Vietnamese accent marks generous vertical clearance and eliminate accidental clipping.
- **Weighted Hierarchy**: Weights are skewed bold (`600`, `700`, `800`, `900`) to guarantee high-contrast legibility across color-flooded tiles and glossy surfaces.
- **Glyph Isolation**: Individual characters and numbers shown for practice utilize `letter-glyph-display` to present letterforms as self-contained learning objects.

## Layout & Spacing

Preschoolers interact predominantly via handheld or tabletop touch screens using gross motor movements. The layout prioritizes ample breathing room, distinct boundaries, and oversized clickable perimeters.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Touch-First Hit Area**: Minimum touch targets are strictly set to 64px (`4rem` / `tap-min`) to eliminate mis-taps.
- **8pt Play Grid**: All dimensional units scale in multiples of 8px (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px), ensuring consistent cadence across game boards.
- **Card-Centric Grid**: Content avoids dense vertical lists. Information is chunked into 2-column or 3-column playful grid layouts on tablets/landscape, and single/dual chunked tiles on mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids sterile drop shadows in favor of a tactile, **toy-brick bevel technique** combined with playful ambient lighting.

### The 3D Pushable Layer (Extrusion)
Interactive elements use solid, saturated color extrusions rather than blurry neutral shadows:
- **Default State**: A bottom offset border/shadow created via `box-shadow: 0 6px 0 0 [Darkened_Base_Color]`.
- **Hover/Float State**: Translates `-2px` on the Y-axis with an increased extrusion of `box-shadow: 0 8px 0 0 [Darkened_Base_Color]`.
- **Pressed/Active State**: Translates `+4px` down on the Y-axis with the extrusion collapsing to `box-shadow: 0 2px 0 0 [Darkened_Base_Color]`, simulating a physical clicky button.

### Ambient Floating Panels
Non-interactive or floating container surfaces (reward modals, exercise trays) utilize soft, tinted under-glows:
- Cards feature a warm 3px outline in `#F0E6D2` or an opaque pastel border, paired with an ambient drop: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.08)`.
- Internal gloss accents are rendered via a translucent white top-half gradient overlay (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)` to transparent).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped and bubbly geometry (Level 3). Edges are radically softened to communicate safety, warmth, and friendliness.

- **Primary Cards & Modals**: Border-radius spans `24px` to `32px`.
- **Buttons, Badges & Counter Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) or hyper-curved squircles (`20px`).
- **Letter Tracing Tiles**: Soft square rounded containers with `24px` corner radiuses and a subtle inset border simulating an indented play tray.
- **Borders**: Chunky `3px` to `4px` solid outlines wrap high-priority UI objects, maintaining clear definition against vibrant scene backgrounds.

## Components

### Chunky 3D Push-Buttons
- **Visuals**: Fully pill-shaped or 20px squircle with an upper specular gloss arc (`radial-gradient` or inset white overlay) and a rich bottom extrusion color.
- **Typography**: `label-chunky` uppercase with generous letter tracking.
- **States**: Includes audio feedback wave icons for audible Vietnamese phonetic cues. Active state physically compresses down into the canvas.

### Flashcard & Letter Tiles
- **Visuals**: Clean cloud-white base (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 4px soft-tinted border (matching the letter's vowel/consonant family color).
- **Internal Content**: Oversized glyph centered with a high-contrast stroke. Top-right corner hosts a mini audio speaker icon pill; bottom-right hosts a celebratory sticker slot.
- **Corner Radius**: `28px`.

### Star Counters & Reward Badges
- **Pill Counters**: Yellow or Coral pill capsule holding a shiny SVG star badge on the left edge with protruding overlap.
- **Text**: `badge-counter` typography in Warm Cocoa with a pure white numeric background pill.

### Audio & Sound Indicator Buttons
- **Purpose**: Essential for preschool phonics navigation.
- **Style**: Circular floating action buttons (minimum 64x64px) with high-contrast Mint Green or Sky Blue backgrounds, pulsating sound-wave ripples during active audio playback.

### Choice Chips & Radio Selectors
- **Visuals**: Replaces traditional checkboxes with big, pressable tactile animal/letter bubbles.
- **Selected State**: Outlined with a vibrant 4px Mint Green rim and an overlaid floating golden star checkmark.

### Congratulatory Toast & Dialog Overlays
- **Visuals**: Full-screen backdrop dimmed with a warm tint (`rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.45)`). Dialog surfaces drop down with a playful bounce spring curve.
- **Embellishments**: Confetti bursts, animated mascot avatars, and dual oversized action buttons ("Học Tiếp" [Continue] and "Nghe Lại" [Replay]).
